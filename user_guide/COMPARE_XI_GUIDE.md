# 🏏 User Guide: Compare Selected XI's

The **Compare Selected XI's** feature is a sophisticated **4-Layer Analysis Engine** designed to simulate a realistic match contest between two specific sets of 11 players. It goes beyond simple career stats to provide a tactical breakdown of how two teams match up *today*, at the *specific venue*.

## 1. ⚔️ Squad Comparison Header (The "Experience" Check)
**Goal:** Instantly evaluate which team is more "heavy" on paper in terms of experience and proven performance.

*   **Start XI Caps:** The total number of international matches played by the selected 11. This reveals the "Depth of Experience".
*   **Avg Caps:** The average experience per player. Useful for identifying if a team is fielding a "Young/Rookie" side vs a "Veteran" side.
*   **Heavy Hitting:** Aggregates **Total Career Runs**, **Centuries (100s)**, and **Half-Centuries (50s)** for the active XI.
*   **Wicket Taking:** Aggregates **Total Wickets** and **5-Wicket Hauls**.

## 2. 📋 The "Pro Stats" Table (The Core Data)
**Goal:** A decision-support table that provides a deep-dive row for every single player, using color-coded signals (Badges) for instant analysis.

### Column Breakdown
*   **Player & Signals:** 
    *   **Role Icons:** 🏏 (Batter), ⚾ (Bowler), or All-Rounder (Both).
    *   **Badges:** Color-coded tags indicating Form and Class (e.g., `RBF-GE` = Recent Batting Form: Great).
*   **Form (Bat/Bowl):** 
    *   Displays the last 5 relevant scores or bowling figures.
    *   *Smart Logic:* It ignores matches where the player was in the squad but did not actually Bat or Bowl, ensuring the "Form" is genuine.
*   **vs Opp (Head-to-Head):** 
    *   The player's specific Average against the **Availability Opposition Team**.
*   **Ven Avg / Ven Wkts (Yellow & Teal Zones):** 
    *   **Yellow Columns (Batting):** The player's specific record at the **Selected Venue**. Highlights "Home Track Bullies".
    *   **Teal Columns (Bowling):** The player's wicket-taking record at this specific ground.

### Visual Cues
*   **Green/Red Text:** Indicates statistical outliers (e.g., avg > 40 is Green, avg < 25 is Red).
*   **Bunny Indicator:** If a "Head-to-Head Nightmare" is detected, a `(🐰 Bunny)` tag may appear.

## 3. 📊 Tactical Matrix (The Archetype Analyzer)
**Goal:** Analyzes how your batters perform against the *Specific Bowling Types* found in the opposition's squad.

### How it Works
1.  **Scans Opposition:** The engine identifies the bowling styles of the opponent's XI (e.g., "Right Arm Fast", "Left Arm Orth", "Leg Spinner").
2.  **Filters Data:** It asks the database: *"How does Batting Team Player X perform specifically against Left Arm Spin?"*
3.  **Outputs Matrix:** A color-coded grid showing **Average** and **Strike Rate** vs those specific threats.

**Why it matters:** 
If the opposition is playing 3 Left-Arm Spinners and your top order averages 12.5 against Left-Arm Spin (Red Box), this highlights a critical tactical vulnerability that general averages would hide.

## 4. ⚔️ Head-to-Head Matchups (The Duel)
**Goal:** Identification of specific player-vs-player psychological edges.

*   **Display:** Two side-by-side lists (Team A Batting vs Team B Bowling AND Team B Batting vs Team A Bowling).
*   **The Bunny Filter:** If a specific bowler has dismissed a batter **3 or more times** in the timeframe, it flags the batter as a **(🐰 Bunny)**.
*   **Detail:** Shows Runs Scored, Balls Faced, Outs, and Average for that specific duel.

## 5. 🔮 Score Prediction (Simulation)
**Goal:** A predictive model for the match context.
*   At the bottom, the engine runs a simulation based on the relative strength of Team A's Batting vs Team B's Bowling (and vice versa) at the specific Venue.
*   **Result:** A projected **1st Innings Score Range** (e.g., "280 - 310").
